Blitz Buggies Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Blitz Buggies is a free cartoon kart-battle game for all ages. We collect as little as possible. You don't need an account, and we never ask for your real name, email address, location or photos.

What the game sends to our server

Only when you play online (solo games against bots never leave your device):

  • Your driver name. It is filtered for rude words. If you leave it empty, the game picks a fun random name.
  • Your kart, driver, paint and hat choices, so other players see your kart.
  • Your game controls and emotes while a match is running.

This information is kept in the server's memory only while your room exists, and is deleted when the room closes. We don't save it to a database, sell it or share it.

Like every website, our server and our hosting provider receive your device's IP address so they can connect you to the game. The game doesn't record it in its logs.

What is stored on your device

  • Your level, coins, hats, daily missions and settings are saved in your browser's local storage.
  • A temporary session code lets you rejoin a match if your connection drops. It is removed when you close the tab.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Talking to other players

There is no text or voice chat. Players can only send four preset emotes (👋 😄 😎 🎉).

Ads

We don't run our own ads, analytics or trackers. When you play on a game website such as Poki or CrazyGames, that website may show ads, and its own privacy policy applies to them.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for children: no chat, filtered names, and no personal information is requested. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/urraf/Blitz-Buggies/issues.
